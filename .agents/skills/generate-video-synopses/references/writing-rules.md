# Writing rules by video type

Apply the common contract first: describe the viewing experience, cover the video's main arc without resolving it, and end with one verified Shirayuki Tomoe quote.

| Type | Include | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talk | Topics that actually recur, the conversational mood, and how comments shape the flow | Turning one passing remark into the main topic; private inference |
| One-shot game | Premise available near the start, play style, emotional or comedic reaction, and non-revealing atmosphere | Culprits, identities, fates, endings, final locations, puzzle answers, or the reason behind a twist |
| Game series | Series or event context, the goal of this installment, familiar play style, and forward momentum | Prior-episode recap that resolves mysteries; late-story discoveries; match results |
| Collaboration | Confirmed participants and the activity that structures the stream | Unconfirmed relationships, off-screen attendance, or chat claims |
| Singing stream | The set's mood, musical range, and talk between songs | Lyrics or a sung line as the featured quote; song outcomes inferred from chat |
| Advice or submissions | Recurring themes and Shirayuki Tomoe's public response style | Names, handles, locations, workplaces, medical details, or another identifying combination |

## Style

- Write natural Japanese in one compact paragraph.
- Prefer concrete verbs such as `味わう`, `相談する`, `進める`, and `振り返る` over promotional superlatives.
- Invite interest by naming the tension, mood, or interaction, not by promising an unsupported emotional effect.
- Keep the body understandable without reading the title.
- Store the quote without `「」`; the UI adds quote marks.
- Preserve public names exactly. Do not normalize a spoken line beyond punctuation and clearly heard orthography.

## Spoiler review

Check the body and quote in context, not only by keywords. Treat these as high-risk when they identify or resolve content: culprit, mastermind, true identity, secret role, death or survival, judgment result, match result, ending, final encounter, late-game character, and puzzle solution. A vague mood such as `不穏` or a non-resolving reaction can be safe when it does not narrow the outcome.

## Quote selection

Choose a line that is:

1. spoken by Shirayuki Tomoe in the target stream;
2. short enough to leave room for a useful body;
3. understandable without revealing a result;
4. characteristic of that stream's humor, emotion, or motivation;
5. linked to the first confirmed second in the evidence.

For singing streams, select the quote from clearly spoken talk between songs. Never reproduce lyrics. For games, films, readings, or TRPG, distinguish Shirayuki Tomoe's own speech from character dialogue, game text, and another participant before accepting the quote.
